Building better spaces with premium aluminium solutions

Boi-Tebo Group is a South African aluminium specialist providing professional design, manufacture, supply and installation of aluminium windows, doors, shopfronts and glazing systems.

Our commitment is to deliver products that combine durability, modern design and precision engineering. Whether it's a residential home, commercial office or industrial project, every installation is completed with quality workmanship and attention to detail.

With extensive experience in aluminium fabrication, site measuring, quotations, project management and installation, we understand the importance of delivering projects on time while maintaining the highest standards.

From enquiry to installation, you deal with one point of contact — not a call centre.

"Our objective is simple: deliver aluminium work that is properly scoped, accurately quoted, professionally installed and clearly communicated."

Our mission
To provide premium aluminium solutions that improve the appearance, security and value of every property.
Our vision
To become one of South Africa's most trusted aluminium companies through quality workmanship, innovation and outstanding customer service.
