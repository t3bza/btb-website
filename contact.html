Quotation terms, in brief

The key terms that apply to every quotation. Full terms and conditions are provided with your written quotation.

Validity
Quotations are valid for 14 days from the date of issue.
Site measurement
All dimensions are subject to final site measurement before manufacture.
Deposit & balance
A 70% deposit is required before production begins. The balance is payable on completion, or before installation, unless otherwise agreed.
Lead time
Manufacturing starts once payment reflects, measurements are approved and specifications are confirmed. Estimated at 10–21 working days, subject to workload and material availability.
Warranty
Workmanship is covered by a 12-month warranty under normal use. Excludes accidental damage, misuse, structural movement and acts of nature.
Ownership
All goods remain the property of Boi-Tebo Group until paid for in full.
Scope
Quotations cover only the items listed. Any additional work or changes are quoted separately.
Cancellation
Orders cancelled after production has started may be charged for work completed and materials ordered.
