How a project moves from enquiry to installation

A straightforward six-step process, so procurement and site teams know what to expect and when.

Enquiry

We receive drawings, schedules, dimensions, photographs or project requirements.

Scope review

We confirm products, quantities, dimensions, finishes, glazing and installation requirements.

Measurement

A site measurement is arranged where required.

Quotation

We prepare a clear quotation based on the agreed scope.

Confirmation

We confirm the approved scope, programme and procurement requirements.

Installation

We coordinate installation and complete the agreed aluminium work.
